## DSP assessment

The proposed normalized shelf coefficients, gain convention and S range agree with the [W3C Audio EQ Cookbook](https://www.w3.org/TR/audio-eq-cookbook/). The cookbook distinguishes shelf slope from Q and describes S=1 as the steepest monotonic setting. This supports the static family; numerical stability of the implemented coefficients and modulated behavior still require the proposed tests.

The plateau blend law is consistent with current `modulation_proportion`. For held p, `1+p*(H_G-1)` is the correct complex response, not the response of coefficients redesigned at the instantaneous gain. The ±12/±6 dB examples and explicit target-curve labeling avoid a misleading UI contract. Fixed poles with bounded p are a sensible realtime design; a fixed-response proof alone does not validate detector/envelope timing or changing-p transients.

The current band uses f64 biquads, while detector levels, dynamics and blend proportions use f32. Preserve Peak operations and ordering exactly. Static coefficient/response accuracy and public f32 sample/envelope accuracy are distinct gates; do not apply the coefficient tolerance to approximate detector logarithms and call a failure noise.

## Implementation requirements and proposal clarifications
