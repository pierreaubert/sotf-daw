# MCRA presence-weight diagnosis

No production p-conditioned gain model was selected. The existing MCRA value p is an exponential average of a threshold crossing above running spectral minima; it is not a calibrated speech/absence posterior.

The original 720 voiced component records remain exactly equal to the committed zero-depth baseline. The additive diagnostic removes the recorded clean source from the same deterministic mixed samples, retains the exact independent noise channel (the speech-removed channel includes ordinary f32 subtraction residue), and checks noise-ranked bins against the independent direct f64 DFT. It records 144 bins, including 128 active-frame pre-gain power checks with maximum scaled error 3.6754e-7 under the unchanged 2e-6 tolerance. Bootstrap snapshots have no valid stored pre-gain power and are explicitly null, matching the existing oracle's active-frame validity rule. Two incorrect trace-read attempts (post-gain buffer, then unavailable bootstrap snapshot) remain archived as failed raw runs; no tolerance changed.

On noise alone in quality mode, full-band power-weighted p is 0.8190 at source-window center 320 ms, 0.9368 at 384 ms, 0.9849 at 469 ms, 0.9988 at 619 ms and 0.999992 at 917 ms. All 166 analyzed bins exceed p=0.5 during those windows. The independent gentle-noise channel reaches weighted p=0.4581 at 917 ms. Low-latency p falls after its shorter physical minimum-window duration; it is still 0.9809 at 304 ms and 0.99987 at 379 ms on the noise step.

These results reject an interpretation of this p as a useful speech/absence posterior on the declared noise step. The proposed conditional-risk equation is mathematically defined and the 175 independent f64 risk/limit/monotonic cases pass, but that does not calibrate its proposed p input. Production p-weighting was therefore not attempted. A separately predeclared model based on the modeled noise-power fraction is investigated under `remaining-denoiser-snr-risk-20261024`; it does not silently turn p into a speech classifier.

`presence-diagnostics.json` contains the final raw run, `noise-presence-summary.json` all 36 full-band summaries, `component-diagnostic-source.rs` the measured additive test, and `risk-derivation.md` the rejected interpretation's explicit assumptions.
